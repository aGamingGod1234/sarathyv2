# Design log

## 01/10/26 | Landing page redesign

Replaced the landing page (`app/LandingPageClient.tsx`, new `components/landing/*`, `components/landing/landing.css`) because it read as a generic stack of card grids with an autoplaying video and a glass nav that rendered noise artifacts. The new page is built around one idea, "chaos to one number": a three.js desk scene (`ChaosScene`, `components/landing/chaos/*`) where card taps, PayNow pings, receipts and coins float, react to the cursor, then land on scroll into three columns that line up with the page (Protected, Day to day, In and saving) and compress into stacks while the safe-to-spend number resolves. That number carries into a dark Money check section where visitors tap purchases and see the verdict, followed by tools with small live visuals, a Future you savings slider, pricing and a closing call to action. All figures come from one example month in `components/landing/example.ts` so they agree everywhere. Removed the hero video and sound control, the liquid-glass nav and shader backdrop styles from `app/globals.css`, and `components/ui/ShaderBackdrop.tsx`. Direction chosen by the agent from Lucas's brief (premium, motion that lines up with the page, interaction, 3D and 2D); not yet reviewed by Lucas.

## 01/10/26 | Hero restored, story-led sections below it

Following Lucas's correction, restored the original hero exactly (video, copy, CTA, sound toggle, audio fade, glass header) in `app/LandingPageClient.tsx` and `app/globals.css`, and rebuilt only what sits below it (`components/landing/BelowHero.tsx`). ## 02/10/26 | Colour system and precision pass

Lucas liked the story direction but found the UI too soft and asked for a researched colour palette that keeps the orange. Added `components/landing/tokens.css`: OKLCH-generated primitive scales with one shared lightness ramp (`scripts/landing-palette.py`), a semantic layer that components use, and a dark-section override so the same rules work on the ink sections. Harmony is split-complementary around the brand saffron: indigo ink replaces plum for text and dark sections, teal replaces mint for "safe", with amber and rose kept distinct from the orange for caution and over. Text pairs pass WCAG AA (body 16:1, muted 5:1). Precision pass: small token radii, hairline borders instead of soft shadows and glows, monospace labels (`fonts.ts`), shorter reveals (`BelowHero.tsx`), and the 3D objects recoloured from the same tokens with tighter shadows (`DayOneStory.tsx`). CSS refactor onto the tokens done by Codex in `landing.css`/`story.css`. Awaiting Lucas's review.

## 03/10/26 | Scroll reveals that read, and options to calm the sections

Lucas noticed there were no reveal animations and asked for tasteful ones, not on everything. The old reveals did run, but a 12px fade starting near the bottom edge finished before anything reached eye level. Rebuilt them in `components/landing/reveal.ts`: each block plays once, as one move, when it reaches about two thirds of the way down the screen. Headlines slide up line by line out of a mask (`Line.tsx`, styles in `landing.css`), then the lead, then the visual. Each section gets at most one signature detail: the Day 9 meter fills, the Day 23 savings line draws, the Day 30 calendar ripples in. Tool and pricing cards step in by row. Reduced motion shows everything with no movement. Fixed the closing number losing its centring during the reveal. Lucas also found the sections busy; three options (trim the words, one idea per section, fewer chapters) are on a comparison page, awaiting his choice.
